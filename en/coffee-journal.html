A simple coffee journal template

A coffee journal can begin with three things: which beans you used, how you brewed them, and whether you liked the cup. The aim is to leave a note that will make sense when you come back to it.

Start with what you want to remember

Write the bean name, the brewer and a rating. Use your own rating scale consistently: a number is a personal reference, not a verdict on the coffee.

Add a short note in your own words

“Would make again”, “too strong for me” or “good with breakfast” is useful. You do not need to recognise a specific fruit or copy the tasting notes printed on a bag.

Keep the settings when they matter

If you want to repeat a cup, note the dose, water and grind setting. Add temperature or brew time if you measured them. Leave an unknown value blank rather than guessing.

Separate the bag from the cup

The bean name and roast date describe the bag. The dose, brewing conditions and rating describe this cup. Keeping them distinct makes a later note easier to interpret.

A template to copy

Beans: [name on the bag]
Brewer: [your brewer]
Rating: [your own score]
Note: [what you want to remember]
Optional: dose / water / grind / temperature / time

Brew Keeps

You can use this template on paper or in a notes app. In Brew Keeps, choose your beans and brewer, rate the cup, then tap “Log it”. Your previous beans and brewer are selected next time; extra details are optional.
