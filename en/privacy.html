Privacy Policy

Effective: October 1, 2026

This policy explains how Okiniiri: Coffee Brew Log (“the app”) handles your information. Your coffee logs are stored only on your device. The developer does not collect your logs.

1. Information stored on your device

The following is stored only on your device and is never sent to the developer's servers:

  • Your brew logs (beans, brewers, water temperature, ratings, notes, etc.)
  • Photos you attach to cups or beans
  • App settings

Deleting the app deletes this information. Backups are exported only when you choose to, and you decide where to save them.

2. Camera and photo access

The camera and photo library are used only when you attach a photo. Selected photos are copied into the app's storage and are not uploaded anywhere.

3. Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC, to show ads. To serve ads, AdMob may collect and use:

  • The advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking
  • Your IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device type, OS version, and ad impressions and taps
  • Diagnostic and performance information

You can change tracking permission at any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. If you don't allow tracking, every feature still works; ads just won't be personalized.

Where required, we ask for your consent to advertising through Google's consent management tools.

For details, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. In-app purchases

The “Remove ads” purchase is handled by Apple through the App Store. Payment information is handled by Apple and is never received by the developer. The app stores only whether the purchase was made, on your device.

5. Sharing with third parties

The developer does not share your logs or photos with anyone. Advertising data is handled as described in section 3.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
