Brew Keeps
Press information

A coffee journal for iPhone to keep beans, brewing notes and personal ratings together.

Product facts

  • All features are free, with ads in the log and reports.
  • Optional one-time purchase removes ads. No subscription.
  • No app account needed. English and Japanese supported.
  • Records stay on the device, with optional sync through the user’s own iCloud.

Availability and local prices are shown in each App Store. EU storefronts are excluded.
