FAQ

Is Brew Keeps free? Do I need an account?

All features are free with ads in the log and reports. No app account is needed. An optional one-time purchase removes ads; there is no subscription.

Can I record coffee offline?

You can save and view coffee records on your device without a connection. iCloud sync, ads and purchase services need a connection.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone and in your own iCloud (when “Sync with iCloud” is on). Nothing is sent to our servers, the developer can’t see your data, and no account is needed.

How do I move to a new phone?

With “Sync with iCloud” on, just sign in with the same Apple ID and install the app — your cups and photos come back.

How do I remove ads?

Settings › Go ad-free. It's a one-time purchase — no subscription.

I paid, but I still see ads

Tap Settings › Restore purchase. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy.

Tapping the stars doesn't save my cup

Rate with the stars, then tap “Log it” at the bottom. If beans or a brewer are missing, the button tells you what's needed.

“What made it good” is empty

Once you've logged enough cups with details like water temp and grind, Brew Keeps shows the conditions linked to your higher ratings. Tap “Add details” when logging to include them.

Can I use Fahrenheit?

Yes. Settings › Temperature unit switches between °C and °F at any time. Past cups are converted automatically.
